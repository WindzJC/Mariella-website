03 / Behind the pages

Meet Mariella
Doose Harper.

Mariella grew up in Clovis, New Mexico, in the 1940s and 1950s. After graduating from Baylor University in 1958, she spent two years traveling abroad. She later met and married Bill Harper, a fellow adventurer.

Over the years that followed, Mariella and her family experienced life across Europe, North Africa, the Middle East, and the Far East. Her memoir looks back on those decades through the perspective of a traveler, wife, and mother.
